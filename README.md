# TLI Research

Finished research and findings on markets, written for both experienced traders and curious beginners. Each document opens with a plain-English verdict, then gives the full evidence and the method behind it.

This repo is research and findings only. It is not financial advice, not a signal service, and not a record of anyone's personal positions or account activity. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

**Start here:** [QUICK_INDEX.md](QUICK_INDEX.md) lists every hypothesis result, grouped by outcome.

**Field note:** [Do Trading Levels Work? 16 Years of Nasdaq Futures vs. Random Zones](field-notes/2026-10-14-do-levels-work.md) summarises the T12, T13 and T16 results in plain English.

Conceptual foundation: Peter Steidlmayer and J. Peter Dalton's Market Profile and auction market theory are referenced and applied throughout as commentary and application, not reproduction.

Part of the Less Intimidating financial-literacy project (thelessintimidating.com).

## Layout
- `hypotheses/` – completed tests of trading ideas
- `cot/` – CFTC Commitments of Traders findings
- `13f/` – quarterly institutional-filing summaries
- `companies/` – company reads
- `field-notes/` – weekly notes
- `frameworks/` – reusable ways of thinking
- `glossary.md` – terms that recur across documents
